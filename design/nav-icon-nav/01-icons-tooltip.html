Direction 01 · Icons + tooltip. Purest form: 18px lucide icons, label revealed on hover and keyboard focus via tooltip (hover is not the only reveal — focus shows it too, so keyboard users are not penalized). Screen readers get the localized label through sr-only text, so aria-selected semantics stay intact. Current pick for Distractions: shield-lock. Tradeoff: maximal minimalism; discoverability rests entirely on the tooltips, and touch devices (no hover) rely on trial-and-error until first use.
